## Why

The problem or opportunity, and who benefits. A few sentences.

## Scope

**In:** what this change delivers.

**Out:** what it deliberately leaves alone, and where that goes instead (a later CR,
or nowhere).

## Design notes

The approach in outline, alternatives considered, and the ADRs this change creates or
relies on.

## Acceptance criteria

Each one testable, including what must *not* happen, and saying how it's verified:
automated (a test or CI check named after the CR and criterion), script, or a manual
check listed in `docs/test-plan.md`.

- [ ] AC1: … *Verified by:* …
- [ ] AC2: … *Verified by:* …

## Constraints and risks

Rules the implementation must respect (dependencies, secrets, platforms), and anything
that could go wrong. "None" is a valid answer.
